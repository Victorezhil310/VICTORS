Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Applicable to VICTORS Web & Android App Suite

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to VICTORS ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users, including children, students, and adult professionals using our PDF tools, mobile utilities, and educational games.

2. Information Collection and Storage

VICTORS operates on a privacy-first model:

  • Local Document Processing: All PDF manipulation, grade calculations, and QR code generation occur locally within your device memory. Files are never uploaded or permanently stored on our servers.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not collect identifiable personal information (such as your name, home address, or phone number) without explicit user consent.
  • Device Information: Technical diagnostics (such as OS version and app crash logs) may be processed in aggregate to ensure app stability across mobile and desktop devices.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Our Kids Corner features interactive math and word games designed specifically for children. VICTORS strictly adheres to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families policy:

  • No personal data is collected from children.
  • No targeted advertisements are displayed in the Kids section.
  • No social features or external communication links are accessible without parental confirmation.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) and local sandboxing within our Android WebViews to prevent unauthorized access or modification of user preferences.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@victorsapp.com.
